I make brands move — and I mean that both ways.

I'm a motion designer and creative strategist based in New York with five years of experience building motion systems, leading brand launches, and delivering high-volume creative work at scale. I've worked across some of the largest brands in the country and some of the most human-centred nonprofits — and I bring the same level of craft and intention to all of it.

At Verizon I spent three years designing motion for one of the world's most recognised brands — social campaigns, web experiences, digital signage, and exec-level motion mockups that shipped to production. I built custom After Effects scripts that saved the team 6–10 hours a week, mentored junior designers, and collaborated directly with creative directors on work seen by millions.

I then moved into healthtech — first at Clover Learning, making complex STEM and healthcare concepts genuinely engaging through 2D and 3D animation, then at PracticeTek, where I operate as the sole motion voice across 12 brands. Most recently I led the full motion suite for Rheo, ChiroTouch's first AI assistant — a MarCom Award-recognised campaign spanning hero video, social, and email.

I'm deeply fluent in the AI tools reshaping creative production and I use them to expand what's possible without sacrificing craft. I build systems that scale, stories that stick, and work that feels like it was made by a human who genuinely cares. Because it was.
