The Brief

Context

ChiroTouch is the leading chiropractic EHR and practice management software in the US. Their primary homepage needed a full redesign — one that would increase conversions and interactivity while more clearly communicating the depth of the product and the outcomes it delivers for practitioners.

The goal was to move beyond static screenshots and make the page feel alive — using motion to demonstrate features in context, guide attention, and build confidence in the product before a visitor ever clicks "Book a Demo."

Lottie Animation

What is Lottie?

Lottie is an animation format that renders After Effects animations natively in the browser — lightweight, scalable, and interactive. It's the standard for high-quality web animation that doesn't compromise page performance.

For ChiroTouch, Lotties were the right choice: smooth, branded animations that load fast and communicate the product's intelligence without requiring video files or heavy assets.

⚡
Hero Animation
The above-the-fold Lottie introduces the product interface in motion — the first thing a visitor sees, designed to immediately communicate speed and intelligence.
