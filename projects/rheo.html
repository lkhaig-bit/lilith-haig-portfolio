The Brief

Context

ChiroTouch — the leading chiropractic EHR software in the US — was launching Rheo, its first AI assistant purpose-built for chiropractors. Designed to handle intake, charting, scheduling, and compliance, Rheo promised up to 92% time savings on documentation alone.

The challenge: AI in healthcare is a hard sell. Practitioners are sceptical, time-poor, and wary of anything that might disrupt their workflow. The campaign needed to feel human-first and approachable — intelligent without being clinical, innovative without being cold.

As the sole motion designer at PracticeTek across 12 brands, I owned the entire motion output of this launch — joining the process early to contribute to the initial pitch deck, designing all storyboards, and then executing the full suite of assets across every channel.

My Role

Responsibilities

This was a full creative ownership project — not an execution brief. I shaped the motion identity from the ground up, then built every asset myself.

03 —

The Orb

Visual Identity

A single mark that
anchored everything.

The Rheo orb — a softly glowing sphere in blush, white, and sky blue — became the visual spine of the campaign. Restrained, warm, and alive, it communicated intelligence without coldness. Every touchpoint, from the hero video to the email headers, was built around its presence.
