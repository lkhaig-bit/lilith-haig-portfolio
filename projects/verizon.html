Three Years at Verizon

Overview

From May 2021 to February 2024, I was a 2D Animator at Verizon — producing motion graphics for one of the largest brands in the world across social media, web, and online advertising. Working within Verizon's strict brand system, I translated graphic design briefs into polished motion across every major channel and platform.

The work ranged from seasonal promotional campaigns to product launches to entertainment partnership spots. Every piece was built to strict brand standards while still finding room for craft and personality within the constraints.

Responsibilities
Produced motion graphics for social, web, and digital signage campaigns
Translated graphic design briefs into polished motion across all formats
Collaborated with creative directors, graphic designers, and web developers
Built custom After Effects scripts that saved the team 6–10 hours per week
Mentored junior designers and supported onboarding
Prototyped motion for digital experiences alongside the web development team

Pay Phone Case Study

NYF Advertising Award

The campaign that
earned recognition.

A case study film produced as part of Verizon's broader brand campaign — demonstrating Verizon's network reliability through the lens of the iconic American payphone. Shot through kinetic typography, graffiti textures, and sticker-collaged black frames, the piece brought a street-culture energy to a corporate reliability message.
